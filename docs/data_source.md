 the data is structured

 Transaction ID - The unique identifier for each transaction
 Item - the product being bought
 Quantity - the number of items bought
 Price Per Unit - the amount of money each item costs
 Total Spent - total amount of money used to purchase the items 
 Payment Method - what method was used to collect or give in the amount of money used to purchase
 Location - where the place is 
 Transaction Date - the day the exchange of goods and money was done
 
 Target would be Total spent with Price per unit and Quantity as the features
